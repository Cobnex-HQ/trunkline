# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and considerate of differing experience levels. Critique code and ideas without personal attacks. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualised conduct and publication of another person's private information are not welcome.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect contributors. Apply these standards consistently in issues, reviews and project discussions. Report private or sensitive concerns through the repository's private reporting channel. Do not amplify private information in a public thread.
